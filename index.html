Our Story

A little farm, a lot of love.

Magnolia Dachshund Co. started the way most good things do — with one dog who stole Tori's heart and never gave it back. What began as a single litter in the farmhouse living room has grown into something Tori is genuinely proud of: a small, intentional operation where every dog is handled daily, socialized with children and other animals, and sent home healthy, happy, and ready to be a true companion.

"I don't breed for volume. I breed for the family that's going to love this dog for the next fifteen years."

Tori lives and works on her family's Mississippi farm, where the long-haired dachshunds roam the yard alongside the rest of life on the land. It's not a kennel — it's a home, and that difference shows in every dog she raises.

Each litter is planned carefully, with health and temperament in mind. When you're ready, reach out — Tori will walk you through what's available and find the right fit.
